# Account Fees & Monthly Maintenance Policy (2026)

## Section 1.1 — Monthly Maintenance Fees
Standard checking accounts carry a $12.00 monthly maintenance fee. This
fee is automatically waived if the account maintains a minimum daily
balance of $1,500, or receives a qualifying direct deposit of $500 or
more per statement cycle.

## Section 1.2 — Overdraft Fees
Overdraft transactions incur a $34.00 fee per occurrence, capped at
three occurrences per business day ($102.00 maximum). Customers may
opt out of overdraft coverage entirely, in which case transactions that
would overdraw the account are declined at no charge.

## Section 1.3 — Savings Account Withdrawal Limits
Savings accounts are limited to 6 outbound transfers or withdrawals per
statement cycle under Regulation D guidance. Excess withdrawals incur a
$10.00 fee per transaction beyond the limit.

## Section 1.4 — ATM Fees
In-network ATM withdrawals are free. Out-of-network ATM withdrawals
incur a $2.50 bank fee plus any fee charged by the ATM operator.
