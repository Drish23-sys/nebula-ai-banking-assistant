# International Wire Transfer Policy (2026)

## Section 4.1 — Domestic Wire Transfers
Domestic outbound wire transfers incur a flat fee of $25.00 regardless of
amount. Same-day processing is available if the transfer is initiated
before 3:00 PM local branch time.

## Section 4.2 — International / Outbound Wire Transfers
Outbound international wire transfers over $10,000 incur a flat fee of
$45.00 plus a 0.2% currency exchange margin on the converted amount.
Transfers of $10,000 or less incur a flat fee of $35.00 with no
additional exchange margin. Funds typically arrive within 1-3 business
days depending on the receiving country and correspondent bank network.

## Section 4.3 — Fee Waivers
Standard wire fees may be waived automatically for customers enrolled in
the Premier Banking tier, or manually approved by a support
representative on a case-by-case basis for account-standing reasons.
Automated systems cannot authorize custom fee waivers; these must be
escalated to a human representative.

## Section 4.4 — Daily Transfer Limits
Retail checking accounts have a default daily outbound transfer limit of
$5,000. Savings accounts have a default daily outbound transfer limit of
$2,000. Limit increases require identity re-verification and are subject
to a 24-hour cooling-off period before taking effect.
