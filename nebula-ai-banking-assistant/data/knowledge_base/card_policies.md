# Card Issuance & Security Policy (2026)

## Section 2.1 — Lost or Stolen Cards
Customers may lock a debit or credit card instantly through the chat
assistant or mobile app. Locking a card suspends all new transactions
immediately but does not close the underlying account. A reference code
is generated for every lock/unlock action for audit purposes.

## Section 2.2 — Replacement Cards
Replacement cards are issued automatically after a permanent card lock
(theft or loss) and are delivered within 3-5 business days via standard
mail, or 1-2 business days with expedited shipping for a $15.00 fee.
The old card number is permanently deactivated and cannot be reused.

## Section 2.3 — Unauthorized Transaction Disputes
Any transaction the customer does not recognize should be reported
immediately. Transactions reported within 60 days of the statement date
are eligible for provisional credit within 10 business days while the
investigation is completed. Reports of unauthorized transactions,
suspected fraud, or a stolen card must always be escalated to a human
fraud specialist and cannot be resolved by the automated assistant
alone.

## Section 2.4 — Card Limits
Standard credit cards carry a default purchase limit determined by
underwriting at account opening. Limit increase requests require a
soft credit check and are reviewed within 2 business days.
